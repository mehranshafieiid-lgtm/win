Lyric display

Timing shifts the lyrics for the current track only. Positive values show lyrics earlier.

Translation

Free machine translation shown under each line. The lyric text is sent to Google Translate’s public web endpoint, with MyMemory as a backup. Either can be slow, rate-limited or unavailable.

Sources & network

The proxy runs on your own server (see proxy.php). It fetches search results, lyrics, translations and audio for the browser, which helps when a service is blocked on the viewer’s network.

Lyrics

Lyrics come from LRCLIB (line-synced). When available, word-synced lyrics are fetched from unofficial community LyricsPlus servers and replace them automatically. Those servers may be unavailable at times.

Track

Saves the audio that is playing right now. Only keep music you have the right to keep.
